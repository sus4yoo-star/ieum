## Business model — for-profit vs non-profit

AMOV runs two kinds of lines under one mission ("Love Moves Everything"):

* **비영리 (non-profit) — ministry & care:** 다바르(DABAR) · 셀라(SELAH) · 만나(MANNA) · 곁에(GYEOTAE).
  Christ-centered / emotional-care. Faith content gates apply (theology-reviewer → Pastor 정운영).
* **영리 (for-profit) — revenue:** 이음(Ieum). A secular B2B SaaS (academy management) that funds the
  mission. No faith/doctrinal content in its product or academy-facing copy unless the founder directs.

## For-profit product — 이음 / Ieum (영리)

AMOV's first for-profit revenue line. A **white-label management app for private academies (학원)** —
secular B2B SaaS, no faith content. Brand idea: 이음 = "connecting" (잇다). Tagline-adjacent: Love Moves Everything.

* **What it does (MVP):** 출결 알림(attendance) → 공지 발송(notices) → 수납 안내(payment). Director presses a
  button; parents get the message on KakaoTalk automatically. Roadmap 2nd: 1:1 학부모 문의, AI 상담. 3rd (upsell):
  Toss 결제 연동, 성적·진도, 시간표·보강, 운영 대시보드.
* **Design principle:** "원장은 버튼만, 나머지 자동" — no manual, no complexity. If it needs a manual, it's wrong.
* **Brand / assets:** name 이음(Ieum), "Bond" logo. Colors **blue #3FA9F2 · green #8CD004 · navy #173050**.
  본명조 (serif) wordmark. (이음 has its OWN palette — do not mix with SELAH navy / MANNA teal.)
* **Domain:** https://ieum.theamov.com (Gabia DNS → Netlify, live). Landing + interactive demo (출결·공지·수납).
* **Target market:** 광명시 소규모 보습·교과 학원, **철산동 우선** (door-to-door + word of mouth + references).
  First-week goal: secure **1 free beta academy** as the first reference.
* **Pricing:** 학부모 무료 / 원장 **학생당 1,500원, 최소 월 40,000원** (beta + lifetime-fixed). Official anchor:
  학생당 2,000원, 최소 50,000원. Examples: 공부방 15명→4만 / 소형 30명→4.5만 / 중형 70명→10.5만 / 대형 120명→18만.
  "평생 고정"은 기본 구독료에만 — 부가 모듈(결제·AI상담·리포트)은 업셀로 ARPU 성장.
* **Sales channel:** 서점·총판 **소개(리퍼럴) 수수료** — 첫 달 구독료 **50%** + 유지 **10%, 영구**. 사장님 부담 0원.
  Trust transfer: 서점이 문을 열고, 클로징·온보딩은 직접. Cold 방문(철산동 10곳)도 병행.
* **Public copy lines (landing):** "이음 · 우리 학원 전용 앱", "학원 운영, 잡무에 치이고 계신가요", "학부모는 무료,
  원장님은 부담 없이". 톤: "검증된 전용 시스템" — "개발 없이" 같은 표현은 쓰지 않음.

### 이음 gates (different from the ministry products)

* **No theology review needed** for 이음 product/academy copy (it's secular). Theology-reviewer is NOT in the loop
  unless faith content is deliberately added. AMOV mission tone (honest, warm, non-manipulative) still applies.
* **Children & data:** academies handle **students' data, many of them minors**. Treat student-facing features and
  any student data handling as sensitive → human review; follow privacy basics. Marketing/sales target 원장 (adults).
* **Honesty:** state pricing exactly as above. Do **not** promise cash-cost freebies that can't be sustained
  (the founder explicitly removed unsustainable free offers; use no-cash perks: 베타 특가, 평생 고정, 셋업비 면제).
* **External sends** (academy/bookstore outreach): prepared fully, founder confirms before sending.

## Audience & voice

* Warm, plain, never manipulative or fear-based. Lead with the emotional hook, not doctrine.
* Church Shorts/card-news skew to Korean women 60–70: large legible type, high contrast, no jargon.
* 곁에 audience = older parents and their adult children. Reassuring, "멀리 있어도 사랑은 닿습니다."

## Compliance lines (non-negotiable in external copy)

* SELAH / MANNA / 곁에 are emotional-companion products — never claim they replace professional
  medical, psychological, or caregiving services. Carry the matching disclaimer above.
* Faith content (non-profit products) → theology-reviewer first; sensitive → Pastor 정운영.
  이음 is secular → no theology review unless faith content is added.
* Children → human review always. For 이음 this means student data and any student-facing feature are sensitive.
* 이음 pricing/claims must match the figures above exactly; no unsustainable free promises.
